# Wizard Shootout 1.0

The first full release. Download `wizard-shootout-v1.0.0-html5.zip` below to host it anywhere, or play it in the browser at https://anugnane.github.io/wizard-shootout/.

## What's in the game

- **Arena duels with bouncing bolts.** Shots ricochet round a maze and can hit you on the rebound. First to five rounds wins.
- **Seven wizard classes**, each with a signature spell and a passive: Arcanist, Pyromancer, Cryomancer, Stonecaller, Stormcaller, Warden and Trickster.
- **Six elemental orbs**: fire burns, ice slows and frosts the floor, earth raises walls, lightning stuns, shield blocks a hit and triple fires a spread.
- **A battlefield that changes**: walls scorch, shatter and appear mid-round. There are ten built-in arenas, and a map editor lets you build and save your own.
- **Modes**: 1 Player vs a bot (Easy, Normal or Hard), 2 Players on one keyboard, Party for 3 or 4 players with gamepads, co-op Survival waves, Online 1v1 by room code or QR, and a Daily Challenge.
- **Your way**: rebindable keys, gamepads, a colour-blind palette, mutators, fog of war, sudden death, a wardrobe of cosmetics and a stats page.

## On a phone

- Play sideways with a joystick that floats under your thumb and thumb-sized FIRE, ORB and PWR buttons. Your first round labels them.
- Add it to your home screen to install it as an app that plays offline. In the browser, a full-screen button hides the address bar.
- The game pauses when you switch apps or turn the phone upright. Sound comes back with one tap, and the phone buzzes when you're hit (Android, can be switched off).
- Every menu works by tap alone, and the HUD stays readable on small screens.

## Balance

The classes were tuned with thousands of bot-vs-bot rounds so that no class's overall win rate falls outside 42–58%.
