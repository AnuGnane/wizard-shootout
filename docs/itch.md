## Description

> **Wizard Shootout** is a top-down arena duel in the spirit of Tank Trouble, but with wizards. Bolts bounce off the walls (your own can hit you on the rebound), orbs scattered round the maze charge your next shots with fire, ice, earth, lightning, shields or a triple spread, and every class has a signature spell on its own button. First wizard to win five rounds takes the match.
>
> **Seven classes.** Arcanist blinks through walls, Pyromancer bursts into flame, Cryomancer frosts the floor, Stonecaller shatters walls, Stormcaller zap-dashes, Warden reflects shots and Trickster scatters a triple spread behind them.
>
> **A maze that changes.** Fire scorches walls, earth orbs raise new ones, ice turns the floor slippery and Stonecaller knocks walls down. Ten hand-built arenas, plus a map editor for your own.
>
> **Modes**
> - 1 Player vs Bot, at Easy, Normal or Hard
> - 2 Players on one keyboard
> - Party: 3 or 4 players with keyboard and gamepads
> - Survival: endless waves of dark wizards, solo or co-op
> - Online 1v1: play a friend with a five-character room code or a QR scan, no account needed
> - Daily Challenge: the same map, mutator and bot for everyone each day
>
> **Play on your phone.** Hold it sideways for a floating joystick and thumb-sized FIRE, ORB and PWR buttons. Your first round labels them. The game has a full-screen button, pauses when you switch apps, and buzzes when you're hit (Android). Add it to your home screen and it installs as an app that plays offline.
>
> All the art and sound are generated in code: pixel art built when the game starts and a Web Audio synth for the music and effects.

## Controls (paste into the description, or the Controls field if the theme has one)

**Keyboard**

| Action | Player 1 | Player 2 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Move | WASD | Arrow keys |
| Shoot | Space | Enter |
| Orb shot | Q | / |
| Signature spell | E | . |
| Pause | Esc | Esc |
| Mute | M | M |

Keys can be rebound in Settings → Controls.

**Gamepad:** left stick or d-pad to move, A shoots, X fires an orb shot, B casts the signature. Gamepads can take any seat.

**Touch:** drag anywhere on the left half to move, FIRE shoots, ORB fires a held orb, PWR casts the signature, and II pauses.
